Support

Write to xia.pengda@gmail.com about a problem, a question or an idea. You can write in English, French or Chinese.

It helps to say which phone and system version you use, what you did, and what happened. Leave private files and details out unless they are needed to understand the problem.

You can also report an issue on GitHub. Reports there are public.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Tinkrum has no account and asks the system to leave its data out of iCloud, computer and Google Drive backups. To keep a copy or move to another phone, export one under Settings → Data → Back up and restore. The backup file is not encrypted: keep it somewhere safe.

I deleted something

A deleted entry stays under Settings → Data → Recently deleted for 30 days.

A change to my app is not what I wanted

Open Change history to undo it or to go back to an earlier version. Changing the app does not delete your entries.

Which AI changes my app?

You choose under Settings → Tink: another AI app on your phone, or Tink, with its built-in rules, the Tinkrum server or your own API key. What each one receives is in the privacy policy.

Does it work without a network?

Yes, apart from changes made by an AI over the network and the downloads that some features need the first time.
